# loomark v2.4.0 Social Copy

## Chinese

loomark 2.4.0 已发布，这一版把顶行重做了一遍。

左边只属于文档：红绿灯之后直接就是标签条。右边是一整列：保存状态和三个按钮在行里，文件面板就在它们下面，按钮和它管的列表待在同一侧。

- **标签占满整行**：标签是一整列纸，从窗口上沿一直落进正文，活动标签就是那一列纸本身
- **标签宽度有了上下限**：短名字不再塌成一条，长名字也不会无限长，名字太多时先压缩再滚动
- **加号不会再消失**：标签占满一行时它仍然在，以前它跟着标签一起滚走了
- **一套细线走到底**：标签之间、行基线、面板边缘是同一个不透明的灰，标签条最左边也有一条
- **切换面板改用 `⌘\`**：菜单和悬停提示同步
- **正文第一行有了呼吸**：首行上方留出 40px，和左右、下方一致

免费、开源，文件永远是最新的。

## English

loomark 2.4.0 is out. This one rebuilds the top row.

The left belongs to the document: the tab strip starts right after the traffic lights. The right is one column: save status and the three controls sit in the row, and the file panel sits directly under them, so the controls and the list they open live on the same side.

- **Tabs fill the row**: each tab is a column of paper running from the window's top edge into the document, and the active one is that column
- **Tab widths have a floor and a ceiling**: short names no longer collapse to a sliver and long ones stop growing; when the row fills up they compress before the strip scrolls
- **The plus no longer disappears** when a full row of tabs would have scrolled it away
- **One hairline per seam**: tab boundaries, the row's baseline and the panel's edge share one opaque grey, and the strip's own start has one too
- **The panel shortcut is `⌘\`** now (it was `⌘⇧B`), and the menu follows
- **The first line of a document gets room to breathe**: 40px above, matching the sides and the bottom

Free, open source, and your files are always current.
