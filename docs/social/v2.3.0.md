# loomark v2.3.0 Social Copy

## Chinese

loomark 2.3.0 已发布，文件面板里的文件夹可以就地展开了。

点一下文件夹，它就在原位往下展开，一层层看到底，不用反复切目录再退回。空文件夹会直接说明它是空的，嵌套再深的文档也只差一次点击。这一版顺手把外壳收紧了一圈。

- **标签从面板的边缘开始**：文件面板打开时标签贴在面板的边界线上，不再是凭空悬在一段灰上
- **拖拽调宽不再是一根橙线**：鼠标按在面板右边缘时，亮起来的就是那根分割线本身，深色主题下也不刺眼
- **名字太长由浮层说全名**：文件列表里的长文件名不再自己左右滑动
- **标题栏按钮用深浅表达状态**：悬停和开启不再闪出一块色块
- **标签更安静**：只显示文档名，不再拖着一个 `.md`
- **启动就是一个标签**：打开应用立刻是一个「未命名」标签

免费、开源、本地优先，支持 macOS、Windows 与 Linux。应用内会自动提示更新，也可以直接下载：

## English

loomark 2.3.0 is out. Folders in the file panel expand in place.

Click a folder and it opens right there, layer by layer, no switching directories and walking back. Empty folders say so, and a document three levels down is still one click away. The shell got a round of tightening along with it.

- **The tab strip starts on the panel's edge**: with the panel open, the first tab sits on its boundary line instead of floating over a grey band
- **Resizing the panel lights up the divider itself**, not an orange highlight, and it reads in dark themes too
- **Long names are spoken by a hover label** instead of scrolling sideways
- **Title-bar controls express state through weight**, no blocks appearing and vanishing
- **Quieter tabs**: the document name only, no `.md` trailing along
- **Launching gives you a tab**: the app opens straight into an untitled tab

Free, open source, local first, on macOS, Windows and Linux. The app offers updates on its own, or download directly:
